I.1

Agriculture Density & Tribe Simulation

Slope, latitude, and altitude filter the landscape into zones of fertility. Clans are born, grow, migrate, merge, and split across the terrain of northern China.

Good farmland is not random. It requires a slope gentle enough to hold soil and water — too flat and drainage fails, too steep and erosion strips the topsoil. The optimal band is roughly 0.1 – 10%. Beyond that, the score falls linearly to zero at 50%.

Latitude compounds the effect. Northern latitudes receive less solar energy, shorten the growing season, and depress yields. Each degree above a reference latitude reduces productivity by an adjustable factor. Altitude adds cold and thinner air: every 100 m of elevation above sea level similarly discounts the base score.

The result is a continuous fertility surface over the landscape — the North China Plain glows green; the Tibetan Plateau and Mongolian steppe are near-zero; the Loess Plateau occupies an intermediate band shaped by slope and altitude together.

Two hundred clans are placed at random on this surface. Each one harvests an exclusive circular territory, grows when in surplus, starves and migrates when resources are exhausted, absorbs weaker neighbours, and eventually fissions when large enough. Nomadic clans drift continuously; sedentary ones stay put until forced out by shortage.

// Base agriculture score per cell
slopeScore  = 0            // slope < 0.1% or > 50%
            = 1.0          // 0.1% ≤ slope ≤ 10%
            = 1 - (slope-10)/40  // 10% – 50% (linear falloff)

latFactor   = max(0, 1 - latReduction × (lat - southEdge))
elevFactor  = max(0, 1 - elevReduction × elev / 100)

agriBase[cell] = slopeScore × latFactor × elevFactor

// Per-frame tribe mechanics
for each tribe:
  owned_cells  = cells within radius where this tribe is closest centre
  harvested    = min(available_in_owned_cells, population × 2 × consume_rate)
  storage     += harvested - population × consume_rate

  if storage < 0:  population ×= (1 - starvation_rate); move
  else:           population ×= (1 + surplus_ratio × growth_rate)

  if nomadic:     move every frame  // 1% pop cost per step

  if dist(A, B) ≤ radius:  larger absorbs smaller
  if pop > 1000:  split chance = log₂₀(pop/1000) × 0.003
