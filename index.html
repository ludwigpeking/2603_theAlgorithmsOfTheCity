Algorithms of the City

Cities are not designed — they compute. Every settlement, road, market, and wall is the output of an iterative process: resources sought, paths worn, beliefs shared, trades agreed. This project makes those algorithms visible, running them on the terrain of Rome to show how geography and decision accumulate into urban form.
